# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 29, 2026**

Halterofit ("we," "us," "our") operates the Halterofit mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

### Account information

- **Email address** — to create and secure your account and to send account-related emails (email confirmation, password reset).
- **Display name** — an optional name you choose, shown in the App.

### Fitness content you create

- Workouts, workout plans, exercises logged, sets, reps, weights, and related training data you enter in the App.

### Photos (optional)

- If you add a profile photo or progress photos, we access your device camera or photo library — only with your permission — to store the image(s) you choose. We do not access your photos for any other purpose.

### Subscription information

- If you purchase a subscription, the purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive your **subscription status** (for example, active or expired). We never receive or store your payment card details.

### Diagnostic data

- Crash reports and technical error data, to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

We do **not** show ads, and we do **not** use advertising or marketing tracking.

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and operate the App (sync your data across devices, keep you signed in).
- Send transactional emails (email confirmation, password reset). We do not send marketing emails.
- Diagnose and fix crashes and errors.
- Process and validate subscriptions.

## Service Providers

We share limited data with trusted providers solely to operate the App:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and cloud sync (data hosted in Canada).
- **Resend** — delivery of transactional emails.
- **Sentry** — crash and error diagnostics.
- **RevenueCat** — subscription management.
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — app distribution and payment processing.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can **delete your account** at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete my account), which removes your personal data and associated content from our systems. You may also contact us to request deletion.

## Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in some EU/EEA countries), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, authenticated access) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Your Choices

- Edit your profile (display name) in Settings.
- Delete your account in Settings.
- Contact us with any privacy question.

## Legal Bases for Processing (EU/UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide and operate the App (your account, cloud sync, subscriptions).
- **Consent** — for optional features you opt into (e.g., profile or progress photos). You may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legitimate interests** — to diagnose crashes, secure the service, and prevent abuse, balanced against your rights and freedoms.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. EEA/UK residents also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

You can exercise the core rights directly in the App (edit your profile, delete your account). For any other request, email **contact@halterofit.ca** and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

## International Data Transfers

Your data is stored and processed by our hosting provider (Supabase) in **Canada**. Canada benefits from a European Commission **adequacy decision**, so transfers of EEA/UK personal data to Canada are permitted without additional safeguards. Our other providers (Sentry, Resend, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) may process limited data in other regions under appropriate safeguards.

## California Privacy Rights

We do **not** sell or share your personal information. California residents may request access to or deletion of their personal information by emailing **contact@halterofit.ca**, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
